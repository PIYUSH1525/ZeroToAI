    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        In a <strong className="text-white">sequential</strong> architecture, agents work one after another like a production line. The output of one agent is the input of the next: research, then write, then review.
      </div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-6">

          </div>
          <div className="mt-2 space-y-2 text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            <div>Easy to understand and debug</div>
            <div>Each step has a clear input and output</div>
            <div>Few messages: one handoff between steps</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-rose-500/25 bg-rose-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-1 flex items-center justify-between">
            <span className="font-bold text-rose-300">Weaknesses</span>
            <span className="rounded bg-rose-950/80 px-2 py-0.5 font-mono text-xs text-rose-300">Slow</span>
          </div>
          <div className="mt-2 space-y-2 text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            <div>Total time is the sum of all steps</div>
            <div>An early mistake flows to the end</div>
            <div>No parallelism</div>
          </div>

      <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
