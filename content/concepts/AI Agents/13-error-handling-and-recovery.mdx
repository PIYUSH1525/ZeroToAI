    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        A <strong className="text-white">retry</strong> simply tries again. Done badly, it makes outages worse. Done well, it hides small glitches from the user. Use <strong className="text-white">exponential backoff</strong>: wait 1 second, then 2, then 4, and so on, with a small random delay (<em>jitter</em>) so many clients do not retry at the same instant.
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
        <strong className="text-emerald-300">In the code:</strong> The flaky tool fails twice and succeeds on the third try. The agent waited 1 s and 2 s between attempts, 3 seconds of simulated time in total.
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-rose-500/20 bg-rose-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
        <strong className="text-rose-300">Never retry blindly:</strong> Do not retry errors that cannot change, such as a bad argument. And never retry an action with side effects, such as a payment, unless it is <strong className="text-white">idempotent</strong> (safe to repeat).
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
