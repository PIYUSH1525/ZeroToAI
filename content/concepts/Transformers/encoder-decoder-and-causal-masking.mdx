    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        Now let us look at <strong className="text-violet-300">Decoder-Only models (GPT-4, Claude, Llama-3)</strong>. Here is the single coolest trick in modern AI—<strong className="text-white">how a Causal Mask lets a Decoder learn $5$ prediction tasks in $1$ single GPU step</strong>!
      </div>
      <div>
        Suppose our training sentence is <code>&quot;AI is super cool&quot;</code>. Because of the <strong className="text-emerald-300">Upper-Triangular Causal Mask ($-\infty$)</strong>, every row of the matrix sees a different prefix of the sentence and predicts the next word simultaneously:
      </div>
      <div className="my-6 rounded-2xl border border-emerald-400/20 bg-emerald-400/[0.04] px-6 py-6 text-center">
        <div className="mb-3 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-emerald-300">The Causal Mask Formula (Applied Before Softmax)</div>
        <div className="text-lg">$$M_{i,j} = \begin{cases} 0 &amp; \text{if } j \le i \quad (\text{Past or Present: Allowed!}) \\ -\infty &amp; \text{if } j \gt i \quad (\text{Future Word: Blocked! } e^{-\infty} = 0) \end{cases}$$</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-2xl border border-blue-400/15 bg-blue-400/[0.05] p-6">

    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        At the <strong className="text-white">Advanced / LLM Engineering</strong> level, two crucial insights explain how modern production Transformers are built:
      </div>

        <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/25 bg-cyan-500/[0.04] p-6">

          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Why are GPT-4, Claude, Llama-3, and DeepSeek all <strong className="text-white">Decoder-Only</strong> instead of Encoder-Decoder? Because in a Decoder-Only model, <strong className="text-emerald-300">$100\%$ of the parameters are used on every token</strong>, and <em>every</em> task—translation, summarization, coding, Q&amp;A—can simply be formatted as <strong className="text-white">next-token prediction</strong> after a prompt! Even simpler: there is only one stack of layers and one KV Cache to manage!
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-amber-500/25 bg-amber-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-amber-300">2. Combining Causal Mask + Padding Mask in Batches</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            In real training batches, sentences have different lengths (so shorter sentences have <code>&lt;PAD&gt;</code> tokens), AND we need the Causal Mask! Therefore, a production Decoder combines <strong className="text-white">TWO masks</strong> using logical AND: block position $(i, j)$ with $-\infty$ if <strong className="text-rose-300">$j \gt i$ (Future Token) OR token $j$ is a <code>&lt;PAD&gt;</code> token</strong>!
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

          <span>⚡ Knowledge Check</span>
        </div>
        <div className="mb-5 text-base font-semibold text-white">
          Why do we use Left-Padding (putting <code>&lt;PAD&gt;</code> tokens on the left: <code>[PAD, PAD, &quot;Hi&quot;, &quot;there&quot;]</code>) instead of Right-Padding when running batched generation with a Decoder-Only LLM?
        </div>

            <summary className="flex cursor-pointer items-center justify-between rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-4 py-3 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 transition-colors hover:bg-white/[0.06] marker:content-['']">
