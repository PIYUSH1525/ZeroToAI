        <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">

        </div>
        <div className="mt-4 rounded-lg bg-slate-950 p-4 text-center text-base">
          <div>$$\text{Final Input Vector} = \text{Word Meaning Vector} + \text{Position Vector}$$</div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        At first, you might think: <em>&quot;Why not just add $+1$ to the first word, $+2$ to the second word, $+3$ to the third word, and $+500$ to the 500th word?&quot;</em>

        Let us see why the simple ideas break down immediately:
      </div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-rose-500/25 bg-rose-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-rose-300">Bad Idea #1: Adding Raw Integers (1, 2, 3 ... 1000)</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Remember that word meaning vectors contain tiny decimals between $-1.0$ and $+1.0$ (like <code>[0.42, -0.15]</code>). If you add $+500$ to the 500th word in an essay, the vector becomes <code>[500.42, 499.85]</code>—<strong className="text-white">the giant position number completely drowns out the meaning of the word</strong>!
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-amber-500/25 bg-amber-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-amber-300">Bad Idea #2: Fractions From 0.0 to 1.0</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            What if you divide by sentence length so the first word is $0.0$ and the last word is $1.0$? In a 4-word sentence, the step between words is $0.25$. In a 100-word paragraph, the step between words shrinks to $0.01$! <strong className="text-white">A 1-word step would mean something totally different in every sentence!</strong>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-2xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-6">

        <div className="space-y-2 text-sm text-slate-300">
          <div>1. <strong className="text-white">Small Bounded Numbers:</strong> All values must stay neatly between $-1.0$ and $+1.0$ so they never crush the word&apos;s meaning.</div>
          <div>2. <strong className="text-white">Consistent Step Size:</strong> The distance from Word #2 to Word #3 must look the same whether the sentence is 5 words long or 5,000 words long.</div>
          <div>3. <strong className="text-white">Unique Pattern per Seat:</strong> Every position needs its own distinct fingerprint vector of length $d_{\text{model}}$.</div>
        </div>

      3. Medium: Sinusoidal Positional Encodings (The Hands of a Clock)
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        How did the creators of the original 2017 Transformer (<em>&quot;Attention Is All You Need&quot;</em>) solve all three rules at once? With a brilliant analogy: <strong className="text-emerald-300">The Hands of a Clock!</strong>
      </div>
      <div>
        Think about how a clock tells time using three hands spinning at different speeds:
        <br />• The <strong className="text-cyan-300">Second Hand</strong> ticks super fast (changes on every single step).
        <br />• The <strong className="text-violet-300">Minute Hand</strong> moves at medium speed.
        <br />• The <strong className="text-emerald-300">Hour Hand</strong> moves super slowly (only changes over long distances).
      </div>
      <div>
        <strong className="text-white">Sinusoidal Positional Encoding</strong> does the exact same thing using smooth <strong className="text-cyan-300">Sine and Cosine waves</strong> ($\sin$ and $\cos$), which naturally stay bounded between $-1.0$ and $+1.0$ forever!
      </div>
      <div className="my-6 rounded-2xl border border-emerald-400/20 bg-emerald-400/[0.04] px-6 py-8 text-center">
        <div className="mb-3 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-emerald-300">The Classic Transformer Wave Formulas (Even vs. Odd Sliders)</div>
        <div>$$PE_{(pos, \; 2i)} = \sin\left(\dfrac{pos}{10000^{2i / d_{\text{model}}}}\right) \qquad \text{and} \qquad PE_{(pos, \; 2i+1)} = \cos\left(\dfrac{pos}{10000^{2i / d_{\text{model}}}}\right)$$</div>
      </div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/25 bg-cyan-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-cyan-300">Early Sliders ($i = 0, 1, 2$): Fast Waves</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Like the <strong className="text-white">Second Hand</strong> on a watch, the first few numbers in the vector wiggle up and down rapidly with every word step—helping the AI tell immediate neighbors (Position #4 vs. Position #5) apart.
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/25 bg-violet-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-violet-300">Late Sliders ($i = 250+$): Slow Waves</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Because of the $10000^{2i/d}$ denominator, the later numbers in the vector wave very slowly—like the <strong className="text-white">Hour Hand</strong>—helping the AI track which paragraph or chapter a word belongs to!
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-cyan-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">
      5. Advanced: RoPE (Rotary Position Embeddings) — How Modern LLMs Work
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        If you open the code for <strong className="text-white">Llama-3, Mistral, DeepSeek, or Qwen</strong>, you will find they do <em>not</em> add position vectors at the start of the model! Why?
      </div>
      <div>
        Think about human language: when you read a sentence, does it really matter if the phrase <em>&quot;blue sky&quot;</em> is at <strong className="text-white">Word #10 and #11</strong> versus <strong className="text-white">Word #510 and #511</strong>? No! What matters is the <strong className="text-cyan-300">Relative Distance</strong> between the two words ($11 - 10 = 1$ word apart)!
      </div>
      <div>
        <strong className="text-emerald-300">RoPE (Rotary Position Embedding, Su et al. 2021)</strong> encodes relative distance with a super clean geometric trick: instead of <em>adding</em> numbers to the word vector, <strong className="text-white">RoPE rotates the word&apos;s vector like the hand of a clock by an angle $m \cdot \theta$ proportional to its seat number $m$</strong>!
      </div>
      <div className="my-6 rounded-2xl border border-cyan-400/20 bg-cyan-400/[0.04] px-6 py-6 text-center text-lg">
        <div>$$\begin{pmatrix} q_1^{\text{rotated}} \\ q_2^{\text{rotated}} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \cos(m\theta) &amp; -\sin(m\theta) \\ \sin(m\theta) &amp; \cos(m\theta) \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} q_1 \\ q_2 \end{pmatrix}$$</div>
      </div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-emerald-300">Why Rotating Beats Adding!</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Remember that Self-Attention compares Word $m$ and Word $n$ by measuring the <strong className="text-white">angle between their vectors</strong> (dot product). If Word $m$ is rotated by $m\theta$ and Word $n$ is rotated by $n\theta$, the angle between them depends <strong className="text-emerald-300">ONLY on their relative gap $(m - n)\theta$</strong>!
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/25 bg-violet-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-violet-300">Context Extension (8k → 128k+ Tokens)</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Because RoPE is just clock rotation, engineers can stretch a model&apos;s context window from $8{,}192$ tokens to $128{,}000+$ tokens (<strong className="text-white">NTK-Aware RoPE Scaling / YaRN</strong>) simply by slowing down the rotation speed $\theta$ so more words fit around the clock face!
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="mt-8 rounded-2xl border border-amber-500/25 bg-[#0A0D16] p-6 shadow-[0_0_25px_rgba(245,158,11,0.05)]">
        <div className="mb-2 flex items-center gap-2 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-amber-400">
          <span>⚡ Knowledge Check</span>
        </div>
        <div className="mb-5 text-base font-semibold text-white">
          In RoPE (Rotary Position Embedding), if Word A at position $m = 10$ is rotated by $10\theta$ and Word B at position $n = 12$ is rotated by $12\theta$, what is the angle difference between them? What if the same two words appear later at $m = 510$ and $n = 512$?
        </div>
        <div className="grid gap-4 md:grid-cols-2">
          <details className="group">
            <summary className="flex cursor-pointer items-center justify-between rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-4 py-3 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 transition-colors hover:bg-white/[0.06] marker:content-['']">
              <span>A) Both pairs have the exact same relative angle difference of 2*theta!</span>
              <span className="text-amber-400 transition group-open:rotate-180">▼</span>
            </summary>
            <div className="mt-3 rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/30 bg-emerald-500/10 p-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
              <strong className="mb-1 block text-emerald-400">✓ Correct!</strong>
              <span>Because $12\theta - 10\theta = 2\theta$ and $512\theta - 510\theta = 2\theta$, Attention sees the exact same 2-word relative distance regardless of where the phrase appears in a 100,000-token book!</span>
            </div>
          </details>
          <details className="group">
            <summary className="flex cursor-pointer items-center justify-between rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-4 py-3 text-sm font-semibold text-slate-200 transition-colors hover:bg-white/[0.06] marker:content-['']">
              <span>B) The second pair is 500 times farther apart in angle</span>
              <span className="text-amber-400 transition group-open:rotate-180">▼</span>
            </summary>
            <div className="mt-3 rounded-xl border border-rose-500/30 bg-rose-500/10 p-4 text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
              <strong className="mb-1 block text-rose-400">✕ Incorrect.</strong>
              <span>Because both vectors are rotated forward together around the circle, the angle <em>between</em> them only depends on their difference $(n - m) = 2$.</span>
            </div>
          </details>
        </div>
      </div>
