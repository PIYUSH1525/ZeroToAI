      3. Medium: The Core Concept — Self-Attention (&quot;Chameleon Words&quot;)
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        How does a Transformer actually understand human language when it looks at all words at once? Through its core engine: <strong className="text-emerald-300">Self-Attention</strong>.
      </div>
      <div>
        Think about how many English words are <strong className="text-white">&quot;chameleon words&quot;</strong>—they completely change their meaning depending on the other words sitting around them! Look at the word <strong className="text-cyan-300">&quot;bank&quot;</strong> in these two sentences:
      </div>

      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="text-xs font-mono uppercase text-emerald-400">Sentence 1 · Nature Meaning</div>
          <div className="mt-1 text-lg font-bold text-white">
            &quot;The <span className="underline decoration-emerald-400">bank</span> of the <span className="text-emerald-300">river</span>.&quot;
          </div>
          <div className="mt-2 text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Self-Attention lets <em>&quot;bank&quot;</em> look across the sentence, spot the clue word <strong className="text-emerald-300">&quot;river&quot;</strong>, and update its vector to mean <strong className="text-white">muddy shoreline</strong>!
          </div>
        </div>

        <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/25 bg-cyan-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="text-xs font-mono uppercase text-cyan-400">Sentence 2 · Finance Meaning</div>
          <div className="mt-1 text-lg font-bold text-white">
            &quot;I deposited <span className="text-cyan-300">money</span> in the <span className="underline decoration-cyan-400">bank</span>.&quot;
          </div>
          <div className="mt-2 text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Here, Self-Attention lets <em>&quot;bank&quot;</em> spot the clue words <strong className="text-cyan-300">&quot;deposited&quot;</strong> and <strong className="text-cyan-300">&quot;money&quot;</strong>, shifting its vector to mean <strong className="text-white">financial vault</strong>!
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div>
        Under the hood, every word creates three vectors—a <strong className="text-cyan-300">Query ($Q$)</strong> (what clues it is searching for), a <strong className="text-violet-300">Key ($K$)</strong> (its label tag for other words to match against), and a <strong className="text-emerald-300">Value ($V$)</strong> (its actual meaning payload)—and runs this single equation:
      </div>

      <div className="my-6 rounded-2xl border border-emerald-400/20 bg-emerald-400/[0.04] px-6 py-8 text-center text-lg">
        <div>$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\dfrac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$</div>
      </div>

      <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-5">
        <div className="mb-2 font-bold text-white">
          How to Read That Equation in 10 Seconds
        </div>
        <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
          1. <strong className="text-cyan-300">$QK^T$</strong> multiplies every word&apos;s Query by every other word&apos;s Key to measure how strongly two words relate.<br />
          2. <strong className="text-violet-300">$\text{softmax}(\dots / \sqrt{d_k})$</strong> converts those raw scores into clean percentages that add up to $100\%$.<br />
          3. <strong className="text-emerald-300">$\times V$</strong> blends the meanings (Values) of the relevant words together according to those percentages!
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
