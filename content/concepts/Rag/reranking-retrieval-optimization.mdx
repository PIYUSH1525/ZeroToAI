    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Think of hiring. A quick screen of thousands of resumes uses simple rules and lets in more people than you will hire. Then interviewers spend real time on the shortlist. You cannot interview thousands, and you should not hire from the rough screen alone. Retrieval and reranking work the same way.</div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-blue-500/25 bg-blue-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-blue-300">Stage 1, retrieve</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">Cheap and wide. Fetch the top 20 to 100 candidates. Goal: do not miss the answer, which means high recall.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-emerald-300">Stage 2, rerank</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">Costly and sharp. Re-score only the shortlist and keep the top few. Goal: put the best passages first, which means high precision.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-blue-500/20 bg-blue-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-blue-300">Why it helps:</strong> the best chunk is often retrieved but ranked fourth or tenth. Without reranking it may be cut off by a small top-k, or buried in the prompt where the model uses it less reliably.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-violet-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">2. Intermediate: Bi-Encoders vs Cross-Encoders</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The difference lies in how the two texts are processed.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">

            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Reads</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Query and chunk separately, each becomes a vector</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Query and chunk together in one pass</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Chunk vectors precomputed</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-emerald-300">Yes, stored in the index</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-rose-300">No, computed per query</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Speed</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-emerald-300">Very fast over millions of chunks</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-rose-300">Slow, so only for a shortlist</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Accuracy</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-amber-300">Good</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-emerald-300">Usually higher, it sees word interactions</td></tr>

          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div>Because a cross-encoder sees both texts at once, it can notice things a single vector cannot: that a negation applies to the question topic, that an answer is about a different product, or that a number matches. Other rerankers include hosted reranking services and prompting a language model to score or order the passages.</div>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">3. Intermediate: The Reranking Pipeline</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">1.</strong> Retrieve N candidates, for example N equals 50, from dense, sparse or hybrid search.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">2.</strong> Score each candidate together with the query using the reranker.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">3.</strong> Sort by the new score and keep the top M, for example M equals 5.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">4.</strong> Optionally drop passages below a minimum score so weak matches never reach the prompt.</div>
      </div>
      <div>The cost is simple to estimate: reranking time is roughly the number of candidates times the time per pair. If a reranker needs about 10 milliseconds per pair, then 50 candidates take about 500 milliseconds when scored one at a time. Batching the pairs on a GPU shortens this a lot, but the principle stands: more candidates improve recall at the price of latency, so choose N from measurements.</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Tuning N and M:</strong> raise N until the right chunk reliably appears among the candidates, then lower it again until latency is acceptable. Keep M small, because only a few high quality passages are needed in the prompt.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">4. Intermediate: Contextual Compression and Retrieval Filtering</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Even a well-ranked chunk usually contains sentences the question does not need. <strong className="text-white">Contextual compression</strong> shortens each retrieved passage to the parts that matter for this question, either by extracting relevant sentences or by asking a model to summarize them. It saves prompt tokens and reduces distraction.</div>
      <div><strong className="text-white">Retrieval filtering</strong> removes whole passages that score below a relevance threshold, or that duplicate each other. Together these steps turn a long list of loosely related chunks into a short, dense context.</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-orange-500/20 bg-orange-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-orange-300">Risk:</strong> compression can delete the sentence that qualifies the answer, such as an exception or a date. Compare answers with and without compression on your test questions before turning it on.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The program stands in for both stages with simple scorers. Stage 1 counts how many words of the query appear in each text, which favors repetition. Stage 2 reads the query and each candidate together: it maps synonyms such as plan to subscription, rewards candidates that cover all query terms, and gives a small bonus when the matched terms are close together. Finally it compresses the winning passage to the sentences that matter.</div>
      <div className="overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060911]">
        <div className="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-5 py-3"><span className="font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-teal-400">rerank_demo.py</span><span className="font-mono text-[11px] text-slate-400">Python 3.10+ · Standard library only</span></div>

          <code>
            <span className="block">synonyms = dict([(&quot;plan&quot;, &quot;subscription&quot;), (&quot;membership&quot;, &quot;subscription&quot;), (&quot;stop&quot;, &quot;cancel&quot;)])</span>
            <span className="block">stop_words = set([&quot;how&quot;, &quot;to&quot;, &quot;a&quot;, &quot;the&quot;, &quot;your&quot;, &quot;and&quot;, &quot;can&quot;, &quot;i&quot;, &quot;do&quot;, &quot;press&quot;, &quot;open&quot;])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">documents = [</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;A&quot;, &quot;Subscription pricing: compare each subscription tier and see how to upgrade a subscription.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;B&quot;, &quot;To cancel your plan, open Settings and press Stop. Cancellations take effect at the end of the billing period. Support can also help by chat.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;C&quot;, &quot;Invoices for every plan are emailed on the first day of the month.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def terms(text):</span>
            <span className="block">    cleaned = &quot;&quot;.join(ch if ch.isalnum() else &quot; &quot; for ch in text.lower()).split()</span>
            <span className="block">    return [synonyms.get(w, w) for w in cleaned if w not in stop_words]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def first_stage(query, text):</span>
            <span className="block">    q = set(terms(query))</span>
            <span className="block">    return sum(1 for w in text.lower().replace(&quot;,&quot;, &quot; &quot;).replace(&quot;.&quot;, &quot; &quot;).split() if w in q)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>

            <span className="block">    q = set(terms(query))</span>
            <span className="block">    t = terms(text)</span>

            <span className="block">    closeness = 1 / (1 + (max(first) - min(first))) if len(first) &gt; 1 else 0</span>
            <span className="block">    return round(coverage + 0.5 * closeness, 2)</span>

            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
