    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Three kinds of mismatch survive simple rewriting.</div>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-violet-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">2. Intermediate: HyDE, Hypothetical Document Embeddings</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>In <strong className="text-white">HyDE</strong>, a language model first writes a short, plausible answer to the question, even without seeing any documents. That hypothetical passage is then embedded and used as the search query. It works because an answer looks like other answers: its vector sits closer to the real answer passage than the short question does.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">1.</strong> Ask the model to write a passage that would answer the question.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">2.</strong> Embed that passage.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">3.</strong> Search the index with it.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">4.</strong> Use only the retrieved real documents as evidence.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/20 bg-violet-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-violet-300">Never use the fake answer as evidence:</strong> it may contain invented facts. It exists only to find real passages. HyDE costs an extra model call and helps most when questions and documents are phrased very differently.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">3. Intermediate: Step-Back Prompting</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div><strong className="text-white">Step-back prompting</strong> asks a broader question before the specific one. For a question about a particular invoice amount, the step-back question might be how invoices are issued and what they include. Retrieving background material gives the model the principles it needs to reason about the specific case.</div>
      <div>The usual pattern retrieves for both: the original specific question and its more general step-back version. The results are merged, so the model receives both concrete facts and the general rules.</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Good fit:</strong> troubleshooting, why questions, and any case where the answer depends on a general policy or concept more than on one specific sentence.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">4. Intermediate: Sub-Question Retrieval</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div><strong className="text-white">Sub-question retrieval</strong> breaks a complex question into several simpler questions that can be searched independently, often in parallel. Each sub-question gets its own evidence, and the model combines the pieces into one answer.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">

          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Sub-question retrieval</td><td className="px-4 py-3">No, they are independent</td><td className="px-4 py-3">In parallel</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Multi-step retrieval</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Yes, later steps need earlier results</td><td className="px-4 py-3">In sequence</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div>A comparison question such as how do the Basic and Pro plans differ is a good example of the parallel kind: one search for each plan, then a combined answer.</div>
    </div>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-cyan-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">5. Advanced: Multi-Step and Iterative Retrieval</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div><strong className="text-white">Multi-step retrieval</strong> is needed when one fact leads to the next. The system searches, reads what it found, forms the next query from it, and repeats. Each round is a small loop: search, read, decide whether the question is answered, and if not, ask for what is still missing.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Search.</strong> Retrieve with the current query.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Read.</strong> Extract the useful fact, such as the name of the owning team.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Decide.</strong> If the question is answered, stop. If not, write the next query.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Limit.</strong> Stop after a maximum number of rounds or when no new information appears.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Costs and risks:</strong> every round adds latency and model calls, and an early mistake carries into every later round. Cap the number of steps, keep a trace of every query and result, and compare against a simple single-search baseline before adopting this pattern.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

    <h2 data-type="code" className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-teal-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">7. Python Implementation: HyDE, Step-Back and a Two-Step Chain</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The program has five short documents and a word-match search. A tiny lookup table plays the role of the language model that writes a hypothetical answer or a broader question. The first two experiments show a failing question succeeding once it is replaced by a hypothetical answer or a step-back question. The third experiment is a two-step chain: it finds the team that owns the billing service, extracts the team name, and uses it to find the manager.</div>
      <div className="overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060911]">
        <div className="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-5 py-3"><span className="font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-teal-400">query_strategies.py</span><span className="font-mono text-[11px] text-slate-400">Python 3.10+ · Standard library only</span></div>
        <pre className="overflow-x-auto p-6 font-mono text-sm leading-7 text-slate-200">
          <code>
            <span className="block">stop_words = set([&quot;how&quot;, &quot;can&quot;, &quot;i&quot;, &quot;my&quot;, &quot;the&quot;, &quot;a&quot;, &quot;that&quot;, &quot;who&quot;, &quot;is&quot;, &quot;of&quot;, &quot;by&quot;, &quot;to&quot;, &quot;get&quot;, &quot;do&quot;, &quot;does&quot;, &quot;for&quot;, &quot;and&quot;])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">documents = [</span>
            <span className="block">    &quot;Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.&quot;,</span>
            <span className="block">    &quot;Invoices are issued monthly and list the plan fee and taxes.&quot;,</span>
            <span className="block">    &quot;The billing service is owned by the payments team.&quot;,</span>
            <span className="block">    &quot;The payments team is managed by Dana Lee.&quot;,</span>
            <span className="block">    &quot;Dana Lee joined the company in 2019.&quot;,</span>
            <span className="block">]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def words(text):</span>
            <span className="block">    return set(w for w in &quot;&quot;.join(ch if ch.isalnum() or ch == &quot; &quot; else &quot; &quot; for ch in text.lower()).split() if w not in stop_words)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def search(query):</span>
            <span className="block">    best = max(documents, key=lambda d: len(words(query) &amp; words(d)))</span>
            <span className="block">    return best if words(query) &amp; words(best) else None</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># A pretend language model: it writes a plausible answer, even without any documents</span>
            <span className="block">hypothetical = dict([(&quot;money back&quot;, &quot;You can get a refund. Refunds are issued to your payment method after the return is processed.&quot;)])</span>
            <span className="block">broader = dict([(&quot;49 dollars&quot;, &quot;How are invoices issued and what do they include?&quot;)])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;1. HyDE&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">question = &quot;How can I get my money back?&quot;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Question search       :&quot;, search(question))</span>
            <span className="block">fake_answer = hypothetical[&quot;money back&quot;]</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Hypothetical answer   :&quot;, fake_answer)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Search with that text :&quot;, search(fake_answer))</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;2. Step-back&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">question = &quot;Why was my invoice 49 dollars?&quot;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Question search :&quot;, search(question))</span>
            <span className="block">step_back = broader[&quot;49 dollars&quot;]</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Broader question:&quot;, step_back)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Broader search  :&quot;, search(step_back))</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;3. Iterative, multi-step retrieval&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def after(text, marker):</span>
            <span className="block">    return text.split(marker)[1].rstrip(&quot;.&quot;) if marker in text else None</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">first = search(&quot;billing service owned by team&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">team = after(first, &quot; owned by the &quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Step 1 finds:&quot;, first, &quot;-&gt;&quot;, team)</span>
            <span className="block">second = search(team + &quot; managed by&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">manager = after(second, &quot; managed by &quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Step 2 finds:&quot;, second, &quot;-&gt;&quot;, manager)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;  Final answer: the billing service team is managed by&quot;, manager)</span>
          </code>
        </pre>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5">
        <div className="mb-2 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-emerald-400">Expected output</div>

          <code>
            <span className="block">1. HyDE</span>
