      3. Medium: Text Cleaning &amp; Normalization (Scrubbing Away the Noise)
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        Raw text extracted from PDFs and websites is full of <strong className="text-rose-300">digital trash</strong> that pollutes your embedding vectors and wastes LLM tokens. Before chunking any document, run it through this <strong className="text-emerald-300">4-Step Cleaning Checklist</strong>:
      </div>
      <div className="grid gap-4 sm:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/25 bg-cyan-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-bold text-cyan-300">1. Strip Repeated Headers, Footers &amp; Page Numbers</div>
          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            Every page of a corporate PDF often repeats <em>&quot;CONFIDENTIAL — ACME CORP 2026 — Page 4 of 90&quot;</em> right in the middle of a sentence that wraps across two pages! Strip repeated header/footer banners so sentences flow cleanly.
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/25 bg-violet-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-bold text-violet-300">2. Remove HTML Boilerplate (Navbars &amp; Footers)</div>
          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            When scraping a webpage, strip <code>&lt;nav&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;footer&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;script&gt;</code>, <code>&lt;style&gt;</code>, and cookie consent banners. Otherwise, every webpage in your database will have identical vectors for <em>&quot;Home | About | Privacy Policy&quot;</em>!
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-amber-500/25 bg-amber-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-bold text-amber-300">3. Fix Broken Line-Wrap Hyphens &amp; Whitespace</div>
          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            PDFs often split words across lines like <code>&quot;reim-\nbursement&quot;</code> and insert 15 blank newlines between paragraphs. Rejoin hyphenated line-wraps and collapse multiple spaces/newlines into clean single breaks.
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-bold text-emerald-300">4. Unicode Normalization (NFKC)</div>
          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            PDF fonts often encode ligatures like <code>&quot;ﬁ&quot;</code> (in <em>&quot;ﬁnance&quot;</em>) as a single weird symbol instead of <code>&quot;f&quot; + &quot;i&quot;</code>, or insert zero-width spaces (<code>\u200b</code>). Running <code>unicodedata.normalize(&quot;NFKC&quot;, text)</code> converts them back to standard characters!
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
