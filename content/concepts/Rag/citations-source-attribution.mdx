  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">3. Intermediate: Generating Citations</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The usual method gives each context passage a short number in the prompt and asks the model to attach the numbers of the passages it used to each statement. After generation, the system replaces the numbers with titles and links.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Label.</strong> Add numbers such as 1 and 2 to the passages in the prompt.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Instruct.</strong> Ask for the supporting numbers after every claim.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Validate.</strong> Reject any number that does not exist in the context, because models sometimes invent citations.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Render.</strong> Convert numbers to readable source names and links.</div>
      </div>
      <div>A stricter form asks the model to <strong className="text-white">quote</strong> the exact supporting sentence. Quotes are easy to verify mechanically by checking that the quoted text really appears in the cited chunk.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">4. Intermediate: Evidence-Based Answers and Provenance</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>An <strong className="text-white">evidence-based answer</strong> makes only claims that some retrieved passage supports. <strong className="text-white">Provenance</strong> is the recorded history behind the answer: which question was asked, which chunks were retrieved and with what scores, which prompt was built, and which answer came out.</div>
      <div>Storing that trail for every answer costs little and pays back quickly. It lets you reproduce a bad answer, audit sensitive uses, and learn from user feedback. It also means that when a document is corrected you can find every answer that relied on the old version.</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-orange-500/20 bg-orange-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-orange-300">Show uncertainty honestly:</strong> if only some claims in an answer are supported, show the supported ones with their sources, and say clearly that the rest could not be verified.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-cyan-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">5. Advanced: Preventing Unsupported Claims</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Asking for citations does not guarantee faithful answers. A model can attach a real source number to a statement the source never made. So add a <strong className="text-white">verification step</strong> that checks every sentence against the passages it cites.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-slate-200">
            <tr><th className="px-4 py-3">Verification method</th><th className="px-4 py-3">How it works</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Trade-off</th></tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Word overlap</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Share of the claim content words found in the cited passage</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Cheap, but fooled by paraphrases and negations</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Entailment model</td><td className="px-4 py-3">A trained model judges whether the passage supports the claim</td><td className="px-4 py-3">More accurate, extra model to run</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Language model judge</td><td className="px-4 py-3">A model is asked to verify each claim against its source</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Flexible, costs a call per answer</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div>Citation quality can be measured. <strong className="text-white">Citation precision</strong> is the share of citations that really support their claim, and <strong className="text-white">citation recall</strong> is the share of claims that have a supporting citation:</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-5 text-center">$$\text{citation precision} = \dfrac{\text{supported citations}}{\text{all citations}} \qquad \text{citation recall} = \dfrac{\text{claims with a supporting citation}}{\text{all claims}}$$</div>
      <div>When a claim fails verification, the options are to remove it, to flag it, or to regenerate the answer with a stricter instruction. In high-stakes settings, prefer removing or abstaining.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

            <span className="block">sources = [</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;1&quot;, &quot;handbook.pdf, page 4&quot;, &quot;Employees receive 20 vacation days per year.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;2&quot;, &quot;faq.md, section 2&quot;, &quot;Unused vacation days carry over for 3 months.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>

            <span className="block">    cleaned = &quot;&quot;.join(ch if ch.isalnum() else &quot; &quot; for ch in text.lower())</span>
