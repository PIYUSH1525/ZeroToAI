    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        The name <strong className="text-white">Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)</strong> sounds like a mouthful of academic jargon, but if you break the three words apart, it describes the exact 3-step recipe of how it works:
      </div>

      <div className="grid gap-4 md:grid-cols-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/25 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-mono text-xs font-bold uppercase text-cyan-400">1. Retrieval</div>
          <div className="mb-2 text-base font-bold text-white">&quot;Go Find the Facts&quot;</div>
          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">

          </div>
        </div>

        <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/25 bg-violet-500/[0.05] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-mono text-xs font-bold uppercase text-violet-400">2. Augmented</div>

          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">

          </div>
        </div>

        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5">
          <div className="mb-1 font-mono text-xs font-bold uppercase text-emerald-400">3. Generation</div>
          <div className="mb-2 text-base font-bold text-white">&quot;Write the Answer&quot;</div>
          <div className="text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">

          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
