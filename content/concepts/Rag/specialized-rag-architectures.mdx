  <section>
    <div className="rounded-2xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300"><strong className="text-cyan-400">The Core Idea:</strong> Searching chunks of text answers many questions, but not all. Questions about relationships need a <strong className="text-white">graph</strong>. Questions about numbers need a <strong className="text-white">database</strong>. Questions across a huge corpus need a <strong className="text-white">hierarchy</strong> of summaries. Questions that touch several documents or systems need <strong className="text-white">routing and synthesis</strong>. Specialized RAG architectures match the structure of the knowledge to the structure of the question.</div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-blue-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">1. Intermediate: Hierarchical RAG</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div><strong className="text-white">Hierarchical RAG</strong> organizes knowledge in levels, such as document summaries on top, section summaries below, and detailed chunks at the bottom. The system searches top-down: first find the relevant documents using their summaries, then the relevant sections, and finally the exact chunks.</div>
      <div>This narrows a huge corpus quickly and helps with questions that need an overview, since higher levels hold condensed information. Approaches such as RAPTOR build a tree of recursive summaries, so the retriever can pick the right level of detail for each question.</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-blue-500/20 bg-blue-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-blue-300">Costs:</strong> summaries must be generated and refreshed when documents change, and a summary can drop details that a question needs. Keep a path from every summary to the chunks beneath it.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-violet-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">2. Intermediate: Graph RAG and Knowledge Graphs</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>A <strong className="text-white">knowledge graph</strong> stores facts as entities connected by relations, for example Billing Service owned by Payments Team, and Payments Team managed by Dana Lee. <strong className="text-white">Graph RAG</strong> retrieves by following those links, so a question that needs several connected facts can be answered by walking the graph instead of hoping that one chunk contains everything.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-slate-200">
            <tr><th className="px-4 py-3">Aspect</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Vector search</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Graph retrieval</th></tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Finds</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Passages with similar meaning</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Entities and their relations</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Multi-hop questions</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-rose-300">Weak, facts are scattered</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-emerald-300">Strong, follow the links</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Setup cost</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-emerald-300">Low</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-rose-300">High, entities and relations must be extracted</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Explainability</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Moderate</td><td className="px-4 py-3 text-emerald-300">High, the path is visible</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div>Some systems also build a graph automatically from text with a language model, then summarize clusters of related entities so they can answer broad, global questions about the whole corpus. <strong className="text-white">Knowledge graph plus RAG</strong> combines both worlds: the graph supplies reliable relations, and text retrieval supplies the detailed passages.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">3. Intermediate: Multi-Document and Multi-Source RAG</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div><strong className="text-white">Multi-document RAG</strong> answers questions that span several documents, such as comparing two contracts. The system retrieves from each relevant document, keeps track of which passage came from where, and lets the model synthesize.</div>
      <div><strong className="text-white">Multi-source RAG</strong> goes further: the knowledge lives in different kinds of systems such as a document index, a database, a wiki and an API. A <strong className="text-white">router</strong> decides which source or sources to query for each question, and the results are merged with their provenance.</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Pitfalls:</strong> sources disagree, have different freshness, and use different identifiers. Label every result with its source, apply conflict rules, and avoid querying every source for every question.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">4. Intermediate: Structured Data and SQL RAG</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Numbers, filters and aggregates belong in a database, not in text chunks. In <strong className="text-white">SQL RAG</strong>, also called text-to-SQL, a model reads the question and the table schema, writes a query, the database runs it, and the result is turned into an answer.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">1.</strong> Give the model the schema: table and column names, with short descriptions.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">2.</strong> The model writes a SQL query for the question.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">3.</strong> Validate the query, then run it with read-only access.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">4.</strong> Pass the result rows to the model, which phrases the answer and shows the query as evidence.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-orange-500/20 bg-orange-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-orange-300">Safety:</strong> allow only read operations, restrict to approved tables, limit rows and run time, and never run a generated query with write permissions. The correct sum comes from the database, so the model must not recompute it by guessing.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 data-type="code" className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-teal-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">7. Python Implementation: A Graph Walk, a SQL Answer and a Source Router</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The program shows three specialized retrievers in a few lines each. First, facts are stored as triples and a walk follows relations from a starting entity to answer a multi-hop question. Second, a small SQLite table answers a numeric question with an exact aggregate. Third, a router inspects the question and picks the right source. The language model steps that would write the query or choose the relations are replaced by fixed rules to keep the example self-contained.</div>
      <div className="overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060911]">
        <div className="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-5 py-3"><span className="font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-teal-400">specialized_rag.py</span><span className="font-mono text-[11px] text-slate-400">Python 3.10+ · Standard library only</span></div>
        <pre className="overflow-x-auto p-6 font-mono text-sm leading-7 text-slate-200">
          <code>
            <span className="block">import sqlite3</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># Graph RAG: facts stored as (subject, relation, object) triples</span>
            <span className="block">triples = [</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;Billing Service&quot;, &quot;owned_by&quot;, &quot;Payments Team&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;Payments Team&quot;, &quot;managed_by&quot;, &quot;Dana Lee&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;Dana Lee&quot;, &quot;reports_to&quot;, &quot;Priya Rao&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;Search Service&quot;, &quot;owned_by&quot;, &quot;Discovery Team&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def neighbors(entity):</span>
            <span className="block">    return [(r, o) for s, r, o in triples if s == entity]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def walk(start, relations):</span>
            <span className="block">    path = [start]</span>
            <span className="block">    current = start</span>
            <span className="block">    for relation in relations:</span>
            <span className="block">        found = [o for r, o in neighbors(current) if r == relation]</span>
            <span className="block">        if not found:</span>
            <span className="block">            return path, None</span>
            <span className="block">        current = found[0]</span>
            <span className="block">        path.append(current)</span>
            <span className="block">    return path, current</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">path, answer = walk(&quot;Billing Service&quot;, [&quot;owned_by&quot;, &quot;managed_by&quot;, &quot;reports_to&quot;])</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Graph walk:&quot;, &quot; -&gt; &quot;.join(path))</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Who does the manager of the billing team report to?&quot;, answer)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># SQL RAG: the question becomes a query over a structured table</span>
            <span className="block">conn = sqlite3.connect(&quot;:memory:&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">conn.execute(&quot;CREATE TABLE sales (region TEXT, month TEXT, revenue INTEGER)&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">conn.executemany(&quot;INSERT INTO sales VALUES (?, ?, ?)&quot;, [(&quot;North&quot;, &quot;March&quot;, 120), (&quot;South&quot;, &quot;March&quot;, 90), (&quot;North&quot;, &quot;April&quot;, 150)])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def total_revenue(month):</span>
            <span className="block">    row = conn.execute(&quot;SELECT SUM(revenue) FROM sales WHERE month = ?&quot;, (month,)).fetchone()</span>
            <span className="block">    return row[0]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;SQL answer, total revenue in March:&quot;, total_revenue(&quot;March&quot;))</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># Multi-source routing: pick the source that fits the question</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def route(question):</span>
            <span className="block">    q = question.lower()</span>
            <span className="block">    if &quot;revenue&quot; in q or &quot;total&quot; in q:</span>
            <span className="block">        return &quot;sql database&quot;</span>
            <span className="block">    if &quot;report&quot; in q or &quot;owns&quot; in q or &quot;manager&quot; in q:</span>
            <span className="block">        return &quot;knowledge graph&quot;</span>
            <span className="block">    return &quot;document index&quot;</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">for question in [&quot;What was total revenue in March?&quot;, &quot;Who manages the team that owns billing?&quot;, &quot;How do refunds work?&quot;]:</span>
            <span className="block">    print(question.ljust(42), &quot;-&gt;&quot;, route(question))</span>
          </code>
        </pre>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5">
        <div className="mb-2 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-emerald-400">Expected output</div>
        <pre className="overflow-x-auto font-mono text-sm leading-7 text-slate-200">
          <code>
            <span className="block">Graph walk: Billing Service -&gt; Payments Team -&gt; Dana Lee -&gt; Priya Rao</span>
            <span className="block">Who does the manager of the billing team report to? Priya Rao</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">SQL answer, total revenue in March: 210</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">What was total revenue in March?           -&gt; sql database</span>
            <span className="block">Who manages the team that owns billing?    -&gt; knowledge graph</span>
            <span className="block">How do refunds work?                       -&gt; document index</span>
          </code>
        </pre>
