  <section>
    <div className="rounded-2xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300"><strong className="text-cyan-400">The Core Idea:</strong> A RAG demo that works on five handpicked questions proves very little. <strong className="text-white">Evaluation</strong> replaces impressions with numbers: does retrieval find the right evidence, does the answer stay faithful to it, and does it actually address the question? Because RAG has two stages that can fail independently, a good evaluation measures both separately, then points to what to fix.</div>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-blue-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">1. Beginner: Why RAG Needs Its Own Evaluation</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>When a RAG answer is wrong, the cause may lie in retrieval, in generation, or in the documents themselves. Judging only the final text hides which.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-slate-200">
            <tr><th className="px-4 py-3">What went wrong</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Final answer looks like</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Where to fix</th></tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">The right chunk was never retrieved</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Wrong or vague</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Chunking, embeddings, retrieval</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">The right chunk was retrieved but ignored</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Wrong, though the evidence was there</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Prompt, model, context order</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">The model added facts of its own</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Fluent but unsupported</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Grounding instructions, verification</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">The source document is wrong</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Faithful but incorrect</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Data quality</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-blue-500/20 bg-blue-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-blue-300">Rule:</strong> evaluate the stages separately first, then the whole system. A single score for the whole pipeline cannot tell you what to change.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-violet-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">2. Intermediate: Retrieval Evaluation</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>These measures compare what was retrieved with what should have been. You already know precision and recall from the retrieval lesson. Two additional measures are especially useful for RAG.</div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/25 bg-violet-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-violet-300">Context precision</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">What share of the retrieved chunks are relevant? Low values mean noise in the prompt.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-emerald-300">Context recall</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">What share of the facts needed for the answer appear anywhere in the retrieved context? Low values mean the answer cannot be produced.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-5 text-center">$$\text{context recall} = \dfrac{\text{needed facts found in the retrieved context}}{\text{all needed facts}}$$</div>
      <div>Other retrieval measures include hit rate, recall@k and MRR. Context recall is the most direct signal that retrieval is the bottleneck.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">3. Intermediate: Generation Evaluation</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Generation quality has three separate aspects, and an answer can pass one while failing another.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-slate-200">
            <tr><th className="px-4 py-3">Aspect</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Question it answers</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Compared against</th></tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Answer relevance</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Does the answer actually address the question?</td><td className="px-4 py-3">The question</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Faithfulness, or groundedness</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Is every claim supported by the retrieved context?</td><td className="px-4 py-3">The retrieved context</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Correctness, or factuality</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Is the answer true?</td><td className="px-4 py-3">A reference answer or trusted facts</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-5 text-center">$$\text{faithfulness} = \dfrac{\text{claims supported by the context}}{\text{all claims in the answer}}$$</div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-emerald-300">Faithful is not the same as correct:</strong> if the retrieved document is wrong or outdated, a perfectly faithful answer is still wrong. That is why correctness needs a reference, and why data quality is part of evaluation.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">4. Intermediate: Golden Datasets and Evaluation Methods</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>A <strong className="text-white">golden dataset</strong> is a set of questions with known good answers and the sources that support them. It is the ruler against which you measure every change.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Collect</strong> real questions from users or logs, covering common cases, rare cases and questions with no answer.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Label</strong> the needed source chunks and a short reference answer, checked by someone who knows the topic.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Extend</strong> with synthetic questions generated by a model from your documents, but review them, since generated questions can be too easy or unnatural.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Maintain</strong> the set as documents and user needs change.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-slate-200">
            <tr><th className="px-4 py-3">Method</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Strength</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Weakness</th></tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Automated metrics</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Fast, cheap, repeatable</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Rough, can miss meaning</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">LLM-as-a-judge</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Scales, understands language, follows a rubric</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Can be biased toward long or first-listed answers and must be checked against humans</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Human evaluation</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Most trusted, catches subtle problems</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Slow and costly, so use it on samples and high-stakes cases</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div>When using an <strong className="text-white">LLM-as-a-judge</strong>, give it a clear rubric, ask for a reason before the score, and compare its scores with human labels on a sample. A judge you have not calibrated is an opinion, not a measurement.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-cyan-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">5. Advanced: Failure Analysis and the Improvement Loop</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Numbers tell you how well the system performs. <strong className="text-white">Failure analysis</strong> tells you why. For each failed question, walk the pipeline from left to right and stop at the first broken stage.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Was the source in the knowledge base at all?</strong> If not, it is a data gap.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Was it retrieved?</strong> If context recall is low, look at chunking, embeddings, filters and query transformation.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Was it ranked high and included?</strong> If not, look at reranking and context selection.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Did the answer use it faithfully?</strong> If not, look at prompts, ordering and verification.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Was the answer complete and relevant?</strong> If not, look at instructions and answer format.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Make it routine:</strong> run the golden set after every change, keep the history of scores, and treat a drop as a failing test. Remember that small test sets are noisy, so a change of a point or two may be chance. Look for consistent improvement and examine the individual questions that changed.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

    <h2 data-type="code" className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-teal-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">7. Python Implementation: A Small RAG Evaluation Harness</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The program holds five chunks and a golden dataset of four questions. Each entry records the chunks that should be retrieved, the facts the answer needs, and what the system under test actually retrieved and answered. For every question it computes context precision, context recall, a word-overlap faithfulness score, answer correctness and a rule-based judge verdict, then assigns a failure type by walking the pipeline from left to right.</div>
      <div className="overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060911]">
        <div className="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-5 py-3"><span className="font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-teal-400">rag_evaluation.py</span><span className="font-mono text-[11px] text-slate-400">Python 3.10+ · Standard library only</span></div>
        <pre className="overflow-x-auto p-6 font-mono text-sm leading-7 text-slate-200">
          <code>
            <span className="block">chunks = dict([</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c1&quot;, &quot;Refunds are issued within 5 business days.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c2&quot;, &quot;Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c3&quot;, &quot;Premium members get free shipping.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c4&quot;, &quot;Support is open Monday to Friday.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c5&quot;, &quot;Passwords must contain 12 characters.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">stop_words = set([&quot;the&quot;, &quot;a&quot;, &quot;is&quot;, &quot;are&quot;, &quot;to&quot;, &quot;of&quot;, &quot;for&quot;, &quot;must&quot;, &quot;do&quot;, &quot;does&quot;, &quot;how&quot;, &quot;many&quot;, &quot;long&quot;])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># Golden dataset plus what the system under test produced</span>
            <span className="block">cases = [</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;How long do refunds take?&quot;, [&quot;c1&quot;], [&quot;5 business days&quot;], [&quot;c1&quot;, &quot;c4&quot;, &quot;c2&quot;], &quot;Refunds are issued within 5 business days.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;How long is shipping?&quot;, [&quot;c2&quot;], [&quot;5 to 7 business days&quot;], [&quot;c3&quot;, &quot;c2&quot;, &quot;c4&quot;], &quot;Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;Do premium members pay for shipping?&quot;, [&quot;c3&quot;], [&quot;free shipping&quot;], [&quot;c2&quot;, &quot;c1&quot;, &quot;c4&quot;], &quot;Premium members pay a flat fee of 5 dollars.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;How many characters must a password have?&quot;, [&quot;c5&quot;], [&quot;12 characters&quot;], [&quot;c5&quot;, &quot;c4&quot;, &quot;c1&quot;], &quot;Passwords must contain 16 characters.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def words(text):</span>
            <span className="block">    cleaned = &quot;&quot;.join(ch if ch.isalnum() else &quot; &quot; for ch in text.lower()).split()</span>
            <span className="block">    return set(w for w in cleaned if w not in stop_words)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
