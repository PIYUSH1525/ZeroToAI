    <div className="rounded-2xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300"><strong className="text-cyan-400">The Core Idea:</strong> Retrieval hands you a pile of chunks. <strong className="text-white">Context construction</strong> decides which ones the model actually reads, in what order, and with what instructions. <strong className="text-white">Grounded generation</strong> means the model answers only from that context, admits when it lacks information, and handles disagreement between sources. This last step often decides whether a RAG answer is trusted.</div>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-blue-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">1. Beginner: From Chunks to a Prompt</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>A RAG prompt has three parts, and the middle one is the retrieved context.</div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-blue-500/25 bg-blue-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-blue-300">Instructions</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">Rules for the model: use only the context, cite sources, say when you do not know.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-violet-500/25 bg-violet-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-violet-300">Context</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">The selected, ordered and labeled passages from retrieval.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-emerald-300">Question</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">The user question, placed clearly after the context.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div><strong className="text-white">Grounded generation</strong> is the practice of tying every statement in the answer to that context. A grounded answer can be checked against the passages; an ungrounded one relies on the model memory, which is exactly what RAG tries to avoid.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-violet-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">2. Intermediate: Selecting Context</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>More context is not better context. Every passage costs tokens, adds delay, and gives the model more to be distracted by. Selection applies a few simple rules in order.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">Threshold.</strong> Drop chunks with a relevance score below a minimum.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">Deduplicate.</strong> Remove repeated or near-identical chunks, which waste space and overweight one idea.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">Diversify.</strong> Avoid filling the context with several chunks that say the same thing. A technique called maximal marginal relevance balances relevance against similarity to chunks already chosen.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-violet-300">Fit the budget.</strong> Add chunks in order of score until the prompt budget is used.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-5 text-center">$$T_{\text{instructions}} + T_{\text{context}} + T_{\text{question}} + T_{\text{answer}} \le T_{\text{window}}$$</div>
      <div>The prompt budget is whatever remains of the model window after the instructions, the question and the space reserved for the answer.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">3. Intermediate: Ordering and Compression</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div><strong className="text-white">Order</strong> matters because models do not use all positions equally. Research on long prompts has found that information in the middle can be used less reliably than information at the start or end. So a common choice is to put the strongest passages at the beginning and the end, and weaker ones in the middle.</div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-slate-200">
            <tr><th className="px-4 py-3">Ordering</th><th className="px-4 py-3">How</th><th className="px-4 py-3">Use when</th></tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/10">
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Best first</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Highest score at the top</td><td className="px-4 py-3">A short context with a clear winner</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Edges first</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Best chunks at the start and the end</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Longer contexts with many chunks</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-4 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Document order</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Chunks in their original reading order</td><td className="px-4 py-3">Passages that must be read as a story</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div><strong className="text-white">Compression</strong> trims each chunk to the sentences that answer the question, so more distinct evidence fits in the budget. Remember the risk from the reranking lesson: compression may remove a sentence that qualifies the answer.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">4. Intermediate: Building a Prompt for Grounded Answers</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The instructions are where grounding is requested. A reliable template contains these elements.</div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Role and scope:</strong> answer only from the context below.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Fallback:</strong> if the context does not contain the answer, say that you do not know.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Conflict rule:</strong> if sources disagree, prefer the newest and mention the older value.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Format:</strong> give a short answer, then cite source ids.</div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-4"><strong className="text-orange-300">Labels:</strong> wrap each passage with an id, its source and date, so it can be cited.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-orange-500/20 bg-orange-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-orange-300">Safety tip:</strong> retrieved text is data, not instructions. Separate it clearly from your own instructions, and tell the model to ignore commands that appear inside the context. A later lesson covers prompt injection in detail.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-cyan-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">5. Advanced: Missing and Conflicting Information</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>Two situations separate careful systems from careless ones.</div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-rose-500/25 bg-rose-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-rose-300">Missing information</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">If nothing in the context scores above the threshold, do not send weak chunks and hope. Either skip generation and answer that nothing was found, or send an empty context with an instruction to say so. Test with questions the documents do not cover.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-amber-500/25 bg-amber-500/[0.04] p-5">
          <div className="mb-2 font-semibold text-amber-300">Conflicting information</div>
          <div className="text-sm leading-7">Two sources can disagree, for example an old and a new policy. Resolve by metadata when you can: newest wins, or the more authoritative source wins. When you cannot, show both values and say they differ.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-5"><strong className="text-cyan-300">Test it deliberately:</strong> keep a test set with unanswerable questions and with known conflicts, and check that the system refuses or flags them instead of producing a confident but unsupported answer.</div>
    </div>
  </section>

    <h2 data-type="code" className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-teal-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">7. Python Implementation: Select, Resolve Conflicts, Order and Build the Prompt</h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>The program takes five retrieved chunks with scores and dates. It drops chunks below a score threshold, removes duplicates and respects a word budget. It then detects conflicting statements, applies the newest-wins rule, orders the chunks with the strongest at the edges, and assembles the grounded prompt with ids, sources and dates. The final line shows the refusal path when nothing strong enough exists.</div>
      <div className="overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060911]">
        <div className="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-5 py-3"><span className="font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-teal-400">context_builder.py</span><span className="font-mono text-[11px] text-slate-400">Python 3.10+ · Standard library only</span></div>
        <pre className="overflow-x-auto p-6 font-mono text-sm leading-7 text-slate-200">
          <code>
            <span className="block">chunks = [</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c1&quot;, 0.91, &quot;2024-03-01&quot;, &quot;handbook&quot;, &quot;Employees receive 15 vacation days per year.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c2&quot;, 0.89, &quot;2025-06-10&quot;, &quot;policy update&quot;, &quot;Employees receive 20 vacation days per year.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c3&quot;, 0.88, &quot;2025-06-10&quot;, &quot;policy update&quot;, &quot;Employees receive 20 vacation days per year.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c4&quot;, 0.74, &quot;2025-01-15&quot;, &quot;faq&quot;, &quot;Unused vacation days carry over for 3 months.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">    (&quot;c5&quot;, 0.31, &quot;2023-02-01&quot;, &quot;newsletter&quot;, &quot;The office picnic is in July.&quot;),</span>
            <span className="block">]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">BUDGET = 22</span>
            <span className="block">THRESHOLD = 0.5</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def count(text):</span>
            <span className="block">    return len(text.split())</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def select(chunks):</span>
            <span className="block">    seen = set()</span>
            <span className="block">    picked = []</span>
            <span className="block">    used = 0</span>
            <span className="block">    for chunk in sorted(chunks, key=lambda c: -c[1]):</span>
            <span className="block">        chunk_id, score, date, source, text = chunk</span>
            <span className="block">        if score &lt; THRESHOLD or text in seen:</span>
            <span className="block">            continue</span>
            <span className="block">        if used + count(text) &gt; BUDGET:</span>
            <span className="block">            continue</span>
            <span className="block">        seen.add(text)</span>
            <span className="block">        picked.append(chunk)</span>
            <span className="block">        used += count(text)</span>
            <span className="block">    return picked, used</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def find_conflict(picked):</span>
            <span className="block">    numbers = &#123;&#125;</span>
            <span className="block">    for chunk_id, score, date, source, text in picked:</span>
            <span className="block">        key = &quot; &quot;.join(text.split()[:2])</span>
            <span className="block">        numbers.setdefault(key, []).append((date, source, text))</span>
            <span className="block">    return [v for v in numbers.values() if len(set(t for d, s, t in v)) &gt; 1]</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def order_edges(picked):</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500">    # put the strongest chunks at the start and the end of the context</span>
            <span className="block">    first = picked[0::2]</span>
            <span className="block">    last = picked[1::2][::-1]</span>
            <span className="block">    return first + last</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">picked, used = select(chunks)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Kept&quot;, len(picked), &quot;of&quot;, len(chunks), &quot;chunks using&quot;, used, &quot;of&quot;, BUDGET, &quot;words&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">for chunk_id, score, date, source, text in picked:</span>
            <span className="block">    print(&quot;  &quot;, chunk_id, score, date, source)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">conflicts = find_conflict(picked)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Conflicting statements found:&quot;, len(conflicts))</span>
            <span className="block">for group in conflicts:</span>
            <span className="block">    for date, source, text in sorted(group):</span>
            <span className="block">        print(&quot;  &quot;, date, source, &quot;-&gt;&quot;, text)</span>
            <span className="block">    print(&quot;  Prefer the newest:&quot;, sorted(group)[-1][0])</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">prompt = [&quot;Answer only from the context. If it is missing, say you do not know.&quot;,</span>
            <span className="block">          &quot;If sources disagree, prefer the newest and mention the older value.&quot;, &quot;Context:&quot;]</span>
            <span className="block">for chunk_id, score, date, source, text in order_edges(picked):</span>
            <span className="block">    prompt.append(&quot;[&quot; + chunk_id + &quot;] (&quot; + source + &quot;, &quot; + date + &quot;) &quot; + text)</span>
            <span className="block">prompt.append(&quot;Question: How many vacation days do employees get?&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">for line in prompt:</span>
            <span className="block">    print(line)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;&quot;)</span>
            <span className="block">no_context = [c for c in chunks if c[1] &gt;= 0.95]</span>

          </code>
        </pre>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5">
        <div className="mb-2 text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-emerald-400">Expected output</div>
