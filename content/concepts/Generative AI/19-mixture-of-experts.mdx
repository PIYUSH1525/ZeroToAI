  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-blue-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">
      1. Beginner: A Team of Specialists
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        In a normal (<strong className="text-white">dense</strong>) LLM, every token goes through <strong className="text-white">every parameter</strong>. Making the model smarter means making it bigger, and a bigger model costs more for every single token.
      </div>
      <div>
        A <strong className="text-white">Mixture of Experts (MoE)</strong> model works like a hospital instead of one doctor. There are many specialists (<strong className="text-white">experts</strong>) and a receptionist (the <strong className="text-white">router</strong>) who sends each patient to the two or three who fit best. The hospital as a whole knows a huge amount, but each patient only uses a small part of it.
      </div>
      <div className="grid gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-blue-500/25 bg-blue-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-1 flex items-center justify-between">
            <span className="font-bold text-blue-300">Dense model</span>
            <span className="rounded bg-blue-950/80 px-2 py-0.5 font-mono text-xs text-blue-300">Everyone works</span>
          </div>
          <div className="mt-2 space-y-2 text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            <div><strong className="text-white">Per token:</strong> Every parameter is used</div>
            <div><strong className="text-white">Cost:</strong> Grows with total size</div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/25 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-6">
          <div className="mb-1 flex items-center justify-between">
            <span className="font-bold text-emerald-300">MoE model</span>
            <span className="rounded bg-emerald-950/80 px-2 py-0.5 font-mono text-xs text-emerald-300">Only some work</span>
          </div>
          <div className="mt-2 space-y-2 text-xs leading-relaxed text-slate-300">
            <div><strong className="text-white">Per token:</strong> The router picks the top few experts</div>
            <div><strong className="text-white">Cost:</strong> Grows with the <em>active</em> size</div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-cyan-500/20 bg-cyan-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
        <strong className="text-cyan-300">Big idea:</strong> MoE separates <strong className="text-white">how much the model knows</strong> (total parameters) from <strong className="text-white">how much it computes per token</strong> (active parameters).
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-violet-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">
      2. Beginner: Where the Experts Live
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        In a Transformer block, the <strong className="text-white">attention</strong> part is usually kept as is. The <strong className="text-white">feed-forward</strong> part, which holds most of the parameters, is replaced by <strong className="text-white">several parallel feed-forward networks</strong>, the experts, plus a small router network in front of them.
      </div>
      <div className="space-y-3">
        <div className="flex items-start gap-4 rounded-xl border border-blue-500/20 bg-blue-500/[0.04] p-4">
          <span className="flex h-8 w-8 shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-blue-500/20 font-mono text-sm font-bold text-blue-300">1</span>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed"><strong className="text-white">Attention:</strong> Runs normally for every token.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="flex items-start gap-4 rounded-xl border border-violet-500/20 bg-violet-500/[0.04] p-4">
          <span className="flex h-8 w-8 shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-violet-500/20 font-mono text-sm font-bold text-violet-300">2</span>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed"><strong className="text-white">Router:</strong> A tiny linear layer scores every expert for this token.</div>
        </div>
        <div className="flex items-start gap-4 rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.04] p-4">
          <span className="flex h-8 w-8 shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-emerald-500/20 font-mono text-sm font-bold text-emerald-300">3</span>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed"><strong className="text-white">Select top-k:</strong> Keep the best $k$ experts (often 1 or 2).</div>
        </div>
        <div className="flex items-start gap-4 rounded-xl border border-orange-500/20 bg-orange-500/[0.04] p-4">
          <span className="flex h-8 w-8 shrink-0 items-center justify-center rounded-full bg-orange-500/20 font-mono text-sm font-bold text-orange-300">4</span>
          <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed"><strong className="text-white">Run and mix:</strong> The chosen experts process the token, and their outputs are blended using the router weights.</div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-amber-500/20 bg-amber-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
        <strong className="text-amber-300">Note:</strong> Experts do not usually specialize in neat human topics like <em>math</em> or <em>French</em>. They tend to specialize in subtler patterns, such as kinds of tokens or syntax.

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-emerald-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">
      3. Medium: Routing in Detail
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        For each token the router computes a score for every expert and turns the scores into probabilities with a softmax. It keeps the top $k$ experts and <strong className="text-white">renormalizes</strong> their weights so they add up to 1. The output is the weighted sum of those experts:
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060913] p-5 text-center text-white">
        $$y = \sum_{i \in \text{top-}k} g_i \cdot E_i(x)$$
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-2xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-xs uppercase tracking-wider text-slate-400">
            <tr>
              <th className="px-5 py-3">Symbol</th>
              <th className="px-5 py-3">Meaning</th>
            </tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/5 text-slate-300">
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">$E_i(x)$</td><td className="px-5 py-3">The output of expert number i for token x</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">$g_i$</td><td className="px-5 py-3">The router weight for that expert (the chosen weights add up to 1)</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">top-k</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Only the k best experts run, usually k = 1 or 2</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
        <strong className="text-emerald-300">Why it saves compute:</strong> If the model has 8 experts and picks 2, only a quarter of the feed-forward parameters do any work for each token.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-orange-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">
      4. Medium: The Load-Balancing Problem
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        A router that is left alone tends to <strong className="text-white">collapse</strong>: it sends almost everything to a few favorite experts, which get better at their job and attract even more tokens, while the others stay untrained. That wastes capacity and makes the busy experts a bottleneck.
      </div>
      <div>
        Training therefore adds an <strong className="text-white">auxiliary load-balancing loss</strong>. It multiplies, for every expert, the <strong className="text-white">fraction of tokens it received</strong> by the <strong className="text-white">average probability the router gave it</strong>, then adds them up. The value is smallest when the load is spread evenly.
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-x-auto rounded-2xl border border-white/10">
        <table className="w-full text-left text-sm">
          <thead className="bg-white/[0.04] text-xs uppercase tracking-wider text-slate-400">
            <tr>
              <th className="px-5 py-3">Fix</th>
              <th className="px-5 py-3">Idea</th>
            </tr>
          </thead>
          <tbody className="divide-y divide-white/5 text-slate-300">
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Auxiliary balance loss</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Penalize uneven token counts across experts</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Capacity limit</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Cap how many tokens one expert may take per batch. Extra tokens skip it or are dropped</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Noise in the router</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Small random noise encourages exploring other experts</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Shared experts</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Some newer designs keep one or two experts that every token uses, plus many routed ones</td></tr>
          </tbody>
        </table>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-amber-500/20 bg-amber-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">
        <strong className="text-amber-300">Rule of thumb:</strong> If an MoE model trains badly, check the <strong className="text-white">expert load first</strong>. A few overloaded experts are the classic sign.
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        MoE models are described with two numbers. <strong className="text-white">Total parameters</strong> decide how much memory you need, because <em>all</em> experts must be loaded. <strong className="text-white">Active parameters</strong> decide how much computing each token costs.

            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">Dense, 13B</td><td className="px-5 py-3">About 13B parameters</td><td className="px-5 py-3">About 13B</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Simple to run</td></tr>
            <tr><td className="px-5 py-3 font-semibold text-white">MoE, about 47B total, about 13B active (for example Mixtral-style 8 experts, 2 per token)</td><td className="px-5 py-3">About 47B parameters</td><td className="px-5 py-3">About 13B</td><td className="px-5 py-3">Knowledge of a large model at the speed of a smaller one</td></tr>

      <div className="rounded-xl border border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] p-5">

        <div className="text-sm leading-relaxed text-slate-300">Experts are often spread across several GPUs (<strong className="text-white">expert parallelism</strong>), so tokens must be sent between GPUs and back. That communication, plus keeping every expert loaded, is the main cost of MoE.</div>
      </div>
      <div className="rounded-xl border border-emerald-500/20 bg-emerald-500/[0.05] p-5 text-sm leading-relaxed">

      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2 data-type="code" className="mb-6 border-l-4 border-teal-500 pl-4 text-2xl font-bold tracking-tight text-white sm:text-3xl">
      7. Python Implementation: A Top-2 Router and a Balance Check
    </h2>
    <div className="space-y-6 text-[16px] leading-8 text-slate-300">
      <div>
        This script builds a router from scratch for 1,000 tokens and 8 experts: it picks the top 2 experts per token, counts how many tokens each expert receives, computes a load-balancing loss, and works out total versus active parameters for an example model.
      </div>
      <div className="overflow-hidden rounded-2xl border border-white/10 bg-[#060911]">
        <div className="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-white/10 bg-white/[0.03] px-5 py-3">
          <span className="font-mono text-xs font-semibold text-teal-400">moe_router.py</span>
          <span className="font-mono text-[11px] text-slate-400">Python 3.10+ · NumPy only</span>
        </div>
        <pre className="overflow-x-auto p-6 font-mono text-sm leading-7 text-slate-200">
          <code>
            <span className="block">import numpy as np</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">rng = np.random.default&#95;rng(0)</span>
            <span className="block">tokens, d&#95;model, n&#95;experts, top&#95;k = 1000, 16, 8, 2</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block">x = rng.normal(size=(tokens, d&#95;model))</span>
            <span className="block">router&#95;w = rng.normal(size=(d&#95;model, n&#95;experts)) &#42; 0.5</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def softmax(z):</span>
            <span className="block">    z = z - z.max(axis=1, keepdims=True)</span>
            <span className="block">    e = np.exp(z)</span>
            <span className="block">    return e / e.sum(axis=1, keepdims=True)</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># 1. The router scores every expert for every token, then keeps the top-k</span>
            <span className="block">probs = softmax(x @ router&#95;w)</span>
            <span className="block">chosen = np.argsort(probs, axis=1)&#91;:, ::-1&#93;&#91;:, :top&#95;k&#93;            # best 2 experts per token</span>
            <span className="block">gates = np.take&#95;along&#95;axis(probs, chosen, axis=1)</span>
            <span className="block">gates = gates / gates.sum(axis=1, keepdims=True)                  # weights of the 2 experts add up to 1</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Token 0 goes to experts&quot;, chosen&#91;0&#93;, &quot;with weights&quot;, np.round(gates&#91;0&#93;, 2))</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># 2. How evenly is the work spread?</span>
            <span className="block">load = np.bincount(chosen.ravel(), minlength=n&#95;experts)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Tokens handled by each expert:&quot;, load.tolist())</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Busiest / average expert load:&quot;, round(float(load.max() / load.mean()), 2))</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># 3. Load-balancing loss: small when the router spreads work evenly</span>
            <span className="block">fraction&#95;tokens = load / load.sum()                              # share of assignments per expert</span>
            <span className="block">fraction&#95;prob = probs.mean(axis=0)                               # average router probability per expert</span>
            <span className="block">aux&#95;loss = n&#95;experts &#42; np.sum(fraction&#95;tokens &#42; fraction&#95;prob)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Balance loss (1.0 means perfectly even):&quot;, round(float(aux&#95;loss), 3))</span>
            <span className="block">&nbsp;</span>
            <span className="block text-slate-500"># 4. Total vs active parameters</span>
            <span className="block text-cyan-300">def moe&#95;params(shared, expert, n&#95;experts, top&#95;k):</span>
            <span className="block">    return shared + n&#95;experts &#42; expert, shared + top&#95;k &#42; expert</span>
            <span className="block">total, active = moe&#95;params(shared=1.6e9, expert=5.6e9, n&#95;experts=8, top&#95;k=2)</span>
            <span className="block">print(&quot;Example MoE: total&quot;, round(total / 1e9, 1), &quot;B parameters, active per token&quot;, round(active / 1e9, 1), &quot;B&quot;)</span>
          </code>
        </pre>
      </div>
