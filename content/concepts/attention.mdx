### 1. What is it?
The Attention Mechanism allows a neural network to focus on specific parts of the input sequence when producing a specific part of the output, rather than treating all inputs equally.

### 2. Why do we need it?
Before attention, models squashed entire sentences into a single fixed-size vector, causing them to "forget" earlier words in long sentences. Attention solves this by looking at the entire sequence at once.

### 4. Mathematical Explanation
The standard Scaled Dot-Product Attention is defined as:

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$

Where:
* $Q$ is the Query matrix (what we are looking for).
* $K$ is the Key matrix (what the input represents).
* $V$ is the Value matrix (the actual content).
* $\sqrt{d_k}$ scales the dot product to prevent vanishing gradients during the softmax step.
